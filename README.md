# ANIMAL BOARDING VALIDATOR
***by ToriLeto13-heart***

Welcome to **Animal Boarding Validator** by ToriLeto13-heart!

This project was designed as a boarding system for animals on Noah's ark. It is one of multiple weekly school projects for my C# class.

Here, you can validate:
-
- **Any** kind of animal
- Up to **50** small animals
- Up to **10** medium animals
- Up to **2** large animals
- **Carnivorous** or **vegetarian** animals

Featuring:
-
- **Boarding assignments** for different sized animals
- **Warnings** to house carnivores separately from prey

Known bugs:
-
- Due to overflows in the Int32 system, you **cannot attempt to board 1 trillion birds**, although I don't know why you'd *want* to.
